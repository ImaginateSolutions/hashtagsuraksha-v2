📄 EXHIBIT A — SUSPECT MESSAGE
FROM: support@amazon-india-help.com
Your Amazon order has been placed on hold
Dear Customer,

Your Amazon order #408-7291836-5523912 has been placed on hold due to a payment verification issue. Your order will be cancelled in 24 hours unless you re-verify your payment details.

Please click here to verify: amazon-india-help.com/verify-payment

— Amazon Customer Service Team

🔎 EVIDENCE CARDS
✓
📮
Sender Domain
Tap to analyze the sender address
The sender is amazon-india-help.com — not amazon.in or amazon.com. Amazon NEVER uses third-party domains for official communications.
🚩 RED FLAG
✓
🔗
Link / URL
Tap to inspect the verification link
The link goes to amazon-india-help.com/verify-payment. The real Amazon URL is amazon.in — this is a spoofed domain designed to steal your payment info.
🚩 RED FLAG
✓
⏰
Urgency Tactics
Tap to analyze the language used
"Cancelled in 24 hours" and "re-verify immediately" are classic urgency tactics. Scammers create panic to stop you thinking clearly.
🚩 RED FLAG
✓
📦
Order Verification
Tap to cross-check order details
Amazon shows all order details inside the app. They would never ask you to re-enter payment info via email. If unsure, log in directly to amazon.in.
🚩 RED FLAG
— Deliver Your Verdict —

✅ Correct — It's a Scam!

All 4 evidence flags pointed to phishing. The fake domain, spoofed link, urgency tactics, and impossible request are classic PHISH moves.

Case closed. You'd make a great cyber detective. The golden rule: always check the actual domain in the URL bar, never click from email.

❌ Not quite — This IS a scam.

Look at the evidence again: fake domain, spoofed link, artificial urgency. These are all hallmarks of a phishing attack.

Don't worry — that's why we practice. The domain "amazon-india-help.com" is NOT Amazon. Always check the URL carefully before taking any action.
