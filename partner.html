What is Hashtag Suraksha?

Five verticals. One mission.

India's national youth cyber-safety movement — five interconnected verticals, delivered to students entirely for free.

🎓

Education & Curriculum

Structured cyber-safety content delivered to students in Grades VI–XII through CASK AI and Surakshak ambassadors.

🤖

Technology at Scale

CASK — our AI cyber-safety assistant — gives students real-time threat recognition, reporting tools, and personalised guidance.

🌐

Community & Culture

Slanglish and The Phish & Chip Show make cyber safety speak the language Gen Z actually uses — campus-native and shareable.

✅

Free, Forever

We reach students entirely for free. Partners make that possible — and gain genuine reach, credibility, and a measurable stake in India's cyber-safe future.
