CERT-In and Cyber Buddha

About CERT-In

The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India has been designated to serve as National agency for incident response under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000. CERT-In operates 24x7 incident response Help Desk for providing timely response to reported cyber security incidences. CERT-In provides Incident Prevention and Response services as well as Security Quality Management Services.

As part of its mandate, CERT-In regularly carries out various activities for development of cyber security capacities, skill building, awareness and citizen sensitization with respect to cyberattacks and cyber frauds. In order to create security awareness within the Government, Public and Private Sector organizations, CERT-In regularly conducts trainings / workshops to train officials of Government, Public and Private sector organizations across all sectors and citizens on focused topics of Cyber Security.

We are pleased to announce the launch of #Suraksha, a national initiative by Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) in collaboration with the Cyber Buddha, aimed at enhancing cybersecurity talent and awareness among students.

About Cyber Buddha

Cyber Buddha is a premier organisation at the intersection of Cyber Safety, Integrated Security and Data Privacy. Combining deep expertise in technology and law, Cyber Buddha designs and delivers impactful programmes that empower citizens, institutions and businesses to thrive securely in the digital age. Our work extends beyond consultancy into national-scale awareness campaigns, educational initiatives and public-private partnerships.

Cyber Buddha has pioneered literature that translates complex cyber and privacy issues into accessible, actionable guidance for the public. Flagship titles such as The Good, The Bad & The Cyber Buddha, Why Smart People Get Hacked? (commissioned and Published by the Ministry of Information & Broadcasting) and The New Age of Privacy (commissioned by National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology, Kohima, Nagaland) have reached diverse audiences, from school children to senior executives, shaping safer online behaviour nationwide.

As the co-creator of #Suraksha, The National Cyber Olympiad, Cyber Buddha brings together government bodies, academia and private partners to build the country’s largest cyber awareness movement. Through its integrated approach and proven track record, Cyber Buddha continues to set new benchmarks in advancing digital literacy, data protection and cyber resilience across India.
