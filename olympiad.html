About the Olympiad

What is NCO?

India's first and only national-level cybersecurity competition built exclusively for school students — testing not just knowledge, but the ability to think, respond, and act in real cyber scenarios. Hover a node to explore.

Three Stages

How the Olympiad works

Each stage is progressively more challenging — from online school-level screening to an in-person national final in New Delhi.

Stage One · School Level
The Online Screening
Open to all registered students. A 60-minute online test covering cyber threat awareness, phishing identification, password security, and digital privacy basics. Top scorers from each school advance.
60 minOnlineMCQ formatSeptember
I
Stage Two · State Level
The State Round
Top performers from Stage I compete at the state level — scenario-based case studies, practical threat analysis, and real-world decision-making. Top 5 per state advance to the National Final.
90 minHybridCase studiesOctober
II
Stage Three · National Final
The National Final
India's best young cyber minds compete in New Delhi. Live scenario challenges, expert panel evaluation, and felicitation. Winners receive national recognition, cash prizes, and mentorship.
In-personNew DelhiLive challengesNovember
